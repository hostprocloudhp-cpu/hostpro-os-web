# Installing HostPro

Send this to the team. Replace the link with your live one before sending.

**App link: https://os.hostpro.agency**

---

## iPhone and iPad

Use **Safari**. Chrome on iPhone cannot install apps — that is an Apple
restriction, not a HostPro one.

1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap **Share** — the square with an arrow pointing up
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

HostPro is now on your home screen. Open it from that icon from now on, not from
Safari — the icon version runs full screen and keeps you signed in.

## Android

1. Open the link in Chrome
2. Tap **Install app** when it appears at the bottom

No prompt? Three dots, top right → **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

If you already have the HostPro APK, it still works. You do not need both.

## Laptop or desktop

Chrome or Edge show a small install icon in the address bar. Click it to get
HostPro in its own window, or just bookmark the tab.

---

## Signing in

Your usual name and password. You will sign in once on this new link, then it
remembers you.

## If something looks wrong

- **Blank screen, or an error with a line number** — screenshot it and send it.
  The error text is the useful part.
- **A change you were told about is missing** — close the app fully and reopen.
- **"No connection"** — you are offline. HostPro needs a network to load
  properties, issues and money.
